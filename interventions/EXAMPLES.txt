PRIMORDIA intervention files
============================
Drop a .json file (one object, or a list of objects) into this folder.  The sim polls
every `sim.intervention_every` ticks, applies it, and moves it to done/ (or failed/
with a .err.txt explaining why).  Nothing here is executed as code.

{"type":"seed_organism","kingdom":"fauna","count":30,"x":50,"y":50,
 "genome":{"diet":0.9,"size":0.7,"speed":0.8}}

  *** READ THIS BEFORE SEEDING INTO AN EMPTY WORLD ***

  Seeded stock normally descends from living residents -- spawn() copies a local animal
  whole, brain included, then the archetype edits the traits you named.  That is why
  seeding works at all: the founders inherit a brain that already knows this world.

  When NOTHING IS ALIVE there are no donors, and the fallback is a RANDOM brain damped to
  fauna.founder_brain_quiet (0.35) with a fourteen-weight instinct prior laid over it.
  That animal wanders more or less at random and eats whatever it happens to stand on.
  Ten consecutive foundings failed this way over years 13,700-19,490, and no adjustment to
  body size, diet, gene loadout, seeding density, graze floor or founder energy made any
  difference, because none of them was the problem.

  The brain is 167 genes named w000..w165, and they are addressable like any other gene.
  To found into an empty world, lift a real animal out of an archive and pass ALL of it:

      import numpy as np, json, io
      SRC  = "state/archive/year_14500"                 # a year the world was alive
      m    = json.load(io.open(SRC + ".json", encoding="utf-8"))
      src  = [g["name"] for g in m["fauna"]["schema"]["genes"]]
      cur  = {g["name"] for g in json.load(
                 io.open("state/checkpoint_latest.json", encoding="utf-8")
              )["fauna"]["schema"]["genes"]}
      z    = np.load(SRC + ".npz")
      gi   = {n: i for i, n in enumerate(src)}
      alive = np.nonzero(z["fauna_alive"].astype(bool))[0]
      row  = alive[0]                                    # pick a healthy adult
      genome = {n: round(float(z["fauna_genes"][row, i]), 4)
                for i, n in enumerate(src) if n in cur}   # skip retired genes

  Seeding jitter adds normal(0, 0.48) to each weight, which is real damage against weights
  that reach 4.0 -- but it is nothing beside a random brain at 0.35 strength.

  Two more things measured the hard way:

  * Use SEVERAL DIFFERENT real animals, not one archetype copied N times.  A crash selects
    repro_threshold upward -- an animal that breeds during a famine gives its energy away
    and dies, one that hoards survives -- so the survivors of any crash are the individuals
    LEAST able to rebuild.  Measured: the bar ran 21.5 -> 39.4 as a population fell, and
    the last two animals held 38.2 energy against a bar of 39.4 and could not breed.  A
    founding population needs a reservoir of low-threshold breeders to ride that out.

  * fauna.founder_energy_mult is 3.5, which lands founders at ~77 energy against a bar near
    21, so every one breeds on arrival and so do its children.  Measured irruption ratios:
    3.5 with random brains, 28x.  3.5 with real brains, 4x.  1.0 with real brains, 1.24x.
    Tune it to 1.0 before founding a herbivore stock into standing crop, and put it back
    afterwards if you are going to seed predators, which is what the 3.5 is for.
